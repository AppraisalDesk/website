Common questions

Pricing, answered plainly.

What counts as an order?

One appraisal order equals one charge, regardless of revisions or resubmissions within that order. If the appraiser needs to make corrections and resubmit, that's still one order.

When do you bill?

We can take our fee out of each order as it closes, or bill you separately at the end of each week. Your choice.

What about the appraiser fee?

Appraiser fees are pass-through and unchanged. The appraiser gets paid their fee either way. What changes is who keeps the management margin: an AMC, or you.

Is there a volume discount?

We're open to conversations at high volume. No published tiers, but if you're processing hundreds of orders a week, let's talk.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. We get you set up and we make money when the platform helps you process orders. That's the alignment: you succeed, we succeed.
