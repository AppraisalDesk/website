Quality

Quality without an AMC in the middle.

Every report is checked before a human ever opens it, and every appraiser is graded on every report. Good work earns the next order. That's how quality runs without an AMC in the middle.

  • • Every submitted report runs through automated checks before a human opens it: completeness, data consistency, photo requirements, compliance flags.
  • • Every appraiser is graded on every completed report, and grades drive assignment priority. Good work earns the next order. Weak work gets fewer chances until it improves.
  • • Rotation by grade and geography means quality is enforced by the system, not by a manager's memory.
