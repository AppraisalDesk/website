Quality

Quality control you don't have to build.

Bringing the desk in-house doesn't mean building a review department. The platform runs the first pass on every report the moment it lands, and every appraiser is graded on every completed job. Your reviewers start at the judgment calls, not the checklists, and good work earns the next order automatically.
