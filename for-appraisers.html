Let's be straight with you

A couple of honest things worth knowing before you sign up.

On order volume: If you're already established with an active license and existing lender relationships, you'll likely see meaningful volume right away, especially in high-activity markets. If you're newer or in a slower market, volume will grow as more lenders come onto the platform. We'd rather be upfront about that than have you sign up with unrealistic expectations.

On payment timing: When you get paid depends on how the order is funded. Borrower-pay orders are typically collected before the appraisal even starts, so your payment will come through within minutes of the lender accepting the order. Lender-pay orders settle on a weekly basis, so it may take a few extra days after the order closes before it hits your account. Either way, you'll always know which type you're dealing with before you accept.

On the platform itself: We're actively building. If you run into something that doesn't work the way you'd expect, or you have an idea that would make your life easier, we actually want to hear it. This platform was built from real AMC operations. The team here listens.

Common questions.

A few things we get asked a lot. If yours isn't here, reach out. We're easy to get ahold of.

What fees should I expect on orders?
Complete transparency: you'll see exactly what you're getting paid before you accept. Some lenders include a technology fee as part of the overall fee structure. If that's the case, you'll see it upfront. No surprises after the fact.
Will AI replace appraisers?
No. Our AI handles things like document scanning and renaming on the order management side, saving our team time so we can run a tighter operation. Your job is valuation. That still requires a licensed professional with local market knowledge, and no software is changing that. Our goal is simple: make receiving and accepting orders as easy as possible so this is one platform you don't dread logging into.
Do I have to accept every order I receive?
Not at all. You see the full order details before committing. Pass on anything that doesn't work for you, no penalty. If the fee is the issue, you can request a change before accepting.
I already work directly with some lenders. What happens to those relationships?
Nothing changes. Appraisal Desk is additive. It's another channel for orders, not a replacement for direct relationships you've already built. If a lender you work with joins the platform, your existing relationship is yours.
Is there a mobile app?
We're mobile-first by design, so the platform works great on your phone through a browser. No app download needed. We believe a well-built web experience can do everything a native app can, and it means we can move faster on improvements without waiting on app store approvals.
How is this different from other platforms I'm already on?
Most platforms require you to build relationships with each AMC or lender individually before you see any work. Here, one signup gets you in front of everyone on the platform immediately. You get paid automatically and faster, especially on borrower-pay orders. You get a clean view of every order, what you earned, and every payout to your bank. And we're building what we think will be the most comprehensive appraiser rating system in the industry, with free performance analysis so you can actually see what's driving your volume and improve it. Most platforms give you a star rating. We're going deeper than that.
