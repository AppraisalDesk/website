About Appraisal Desk

Built by operators, not observers.

Most appraisal software was built by technology companies that studied the industry. This platform was built inside a live appraisal operation, by people who spent 15 years running appraisal desks. That's a different starting point, and it shows in the product.

Where this came from

Built in a real operation. Now available to everyone.

Appraisal Desk started as internal tooling inside a high-volume appraisal operation. It ran hundreds of orders a week in production for 15 years before anyone else could buy it.

Today, Appraisal Desk is a dedicated software company, and that operation is our anchor customer: 20,000 appraisals a year, still running on the platform every day. Same builders. Same platform. Our only job now is making it better.

15 years inside a live operation Not studying appraisal management from the outside. Fifteen years inside it: thousands of orders a year, real appraisers, real lenders, and real consequences when something slipped.
Built in production, not a lab Every feature was stress-tested in a live operation before it shipped. You don't inherit beta software. You inherit something that already works at scale.
A software company. This is all we do. We don't manage appraisals. We build the platform that desks of every kind run on: credit unions, lenders, and AMCs.

Proven in a real, high-volume operation long before it was sold as software. It doesn't learn on your orders.
