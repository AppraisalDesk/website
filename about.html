About Appraisal Desk

Built by operators, not observers.

Most appraisal software was built by technology companies that studied the industry. We built ours by running an AMC for 15 years. That's a different starting point, and it shows in the product.

Where this came from

The software that ran our AMC. Now available to everyone else.

Appraisal Desk started as internal tooling. We built it to run our own AMC operations, not to sell it. It handled hundreds of orders a week in production for years before we made it available externally.

When we decided to offer it to other teams, we spun out Appraisal Desk as its own company. Same team. Same platform. Our only job now is making it better.

15+ years of AMC operations

Not studying appraisal management. Actually doing it. Thousands of orders annually, real appraisers, real lenders, real consequences when things went wrong.

Built in production, not a lab

Every feature was stress-tested in a real operation before it shipped. You don't inherit beta software. You inherit something that already works at scale.

A spinout, not a pivot

Appraisal Desk is a dedicated technology company. This is all we do.
