The core insight

Paying appraisers quickly is a competitive advantage. Nobody is using it.

Every platform in this industry moves money the same way: collect it, take a cut, move on. The appraiser gets paid weeks later. Nobody has fixed this because nobody tracks the money all the way through. We do.

The problem

Appraisers notice who pays them fast and who makes them wait. That shows up in the quality and priority of the work they send back to you. This is why appraiser quality varies more than it should.

How Appraisal Desk fixes it

When a lender approves an order, the appraiser gets paid. Automatically. No manual steps. No waiting on a check. Nobody is floating cash. The whole transaction closes inside the platform.

What this means for you

The lenders using Appraisal Desk are building a reputation with appraisers as the desk that pays on time. Better appraisers prioritize your orders. That's a real edge.
