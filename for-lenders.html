What changes

Nobody babysits an order here.

Every appraisal order drags a pile of manual work behind it. Status calls. Deadline checks. Document renaming. Payment collection. We built the platform so nobody on your team does any of it.

  • Nobody makes status calls. The platform updates everyone waiting on the order automatically, including your loan officers. The "any word on the appraisal?" calls just stop.
  • Nobody babysits deadlines. The platform watches every active file and flags the one that's about to slip before it slips: an appraiser who hasn't accepted, a report coming up on its due date, a gap about to become a delay.
  • Nobody renames PDFs. Incoming documents are read, renamed, and filed automatically.
  • Nobody chases payments. Borrower payments are collected up front, and every charge, adjustment, and payout is tracked in the platform from start to finish.
  • Nobody builds a panel by hand. Assignment runs automatically by grade and geography, so the right local appraiser sees the order without anyone playing matchmaker.
  • Nobody runs first-pass QC by eye. QC is quality control, the review every report goes through before it counts. The platform runs the first pass on every report before a human opens it, so your reviewers spend their time on judgment calls, not checklists.

Your loan officers stop calling the desk.

Every update an order generates goes out automatically to the people waiting on it. Your LOs see where their appraisal stands without asking. Your processors stop playing middleman. Your desk gets its day back.

When something actually needs a human, the platform says so. Everything else just moves.

The back office

Billing that doesn't live in your inbox.

Email billing still exists in this industry. We don't do it.

Borrowers pay by card up front through the platform. Fees, adjustments, refunds, and appraiser payouts are all tracked in one place, tied to the order they belong to. When accounting closes the month, the numbers are already reconciled and ready to export. No invoice hunting, no spreadsheet stitching.

The panel

The appraiser panel comes with it.

Your panel is the roster of appraisers who take your orders, and building one from scratch is usually the scariest part of running a desk. On Appraisal Desk, you're not starting from zero. Thousands of vetted appraisers are already on the platform, across all 50 states, and automated rotation puts your orders in front of qualified local appraisers from day one.

Already have appraisers you trust? Bring them. Your appraisers stay first in your rotation, and the wider network fills the gaps in your coverage.

Quality

Quality checks on every report, before anyone opens it.

Every report gets a first-pass review the moment it lands: completeness, common errors, the things a reviewer catches on their third coffee. Problems route back to the appraiser with clear notes. Your reviewers spend their time on the calls that need judgment.

And when your examiner asks, the answers are already there. Every order carries its full history automatically: who was assigned, when, what changed, and why. Appraiser independence is enforced inside the workflow itself, not by memo. Export what you need in minutes.

UAD 3.6

UAD 3.6 ready, without the scramble.

UAD 3.6 is the biggest change to appraisal reporting in decades: the familiar forms go away, replaced by one dynamic, data-driven report. It becomes mandatory in November 2026, which means every lender's appraisal process has to change this year.

Appraisal Desk is ready now. Orders, products, and reports already run the new way. And your team doesn't relearn anything: presets let them order "a 1004" like they always have while the platform handles the new format underneath. Make the transition once, and stop thinking about it.
