The back office

Email billing still exists in this industry. We don't do it.

Every charge, every adjustment, every payout is tracked in the platform automatically. You'll have a full financial trail before anyone asks for one.

  • • Collect payment from borrowers or lenders. Each order configurable for who pays, in what split.
  • • Hold funds during the order lifecycle. Pay out the appraiser when the lender approves.
  • • Track every fee and every price change: who, when, how much.
  • • Issue refunds, manage credit card fees, generate financial reports, issue 1099s.
  • • Every party logs in and sees exactly what was charged, adjusted, and paid.

No chasing payments. No reconciling outside the platform. Hours back every week.
